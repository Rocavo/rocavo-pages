Rocavo tools

Small, free tools that each do one job. Each one runs in your browser, names the rule or calculation it relies on and the date we last checked it, and says what it does not cover.

  • HIPAA records request deadline calculator
    The last day to act on a patient's records request under the 30-day limit and the new 15-calendar-day limit, the extension dates, and an extension letter to adapt.
  • HIPAA fee schedule page builder
    Writes the fee schedule that practices charging for copies of records must post under new 45 CFR 164.525, from fees you type in.
